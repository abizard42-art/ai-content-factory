# Character Consistency — Multi-channel

## Core rule
Never carry a character design from one channel into another unless the user explicitly approves it. Each channel has its own visual identity.

## Sejarah Dunia — Raka
Raka is the recurring 2D anime-style male host:
- youthful appearance;
- warm medium-tan skin;
- large brown eyes;
- thick, tousled black hair;
- friendly, expressive face and open smile;
- tan-brown explorer jacket over a black shirt;
- compass pendant necklace.

The saved reference image of Raka in front of the Giza pyramids is the canonical visual benchmark. When the image workflow supports reference images, use that approved reference.

Keep his face, apparent age, skin tone, eyes, hairstyle, outfit, and compass pendant consistent. Raka is a selective presenter, not a permanent talking head; do not force him into every scene.

## Peta Sunyi — minimalist stickman
The recurring main character for Peta Sunyi is a plain, minimalist stickman. It must look clearly different from Raka and must not inherit Raka's anime features, clothing, accessories, or detailed face.

### Canonical design
- simple round head and clean, thin line-art body/limbs;
- plain monochrome treatment, preferably off-white/white lines against darker scenes or dark lines on light backgrounds when contrast requires it;
- no hair, clothing, shoes, props, jewellery, or signature accessories;
- face kept blank or limited to tiny, simple marks only when an emotion is necessary;
- consistent head-to-body proportions and line thickness across episodes;
- restrained, cinematic line-art finish rather than a cute/chibi mascot or a detailed cartoon person.

### Performance and placement
- Communicate emotion through posture, silhouette, spacing, and movement rather than elaborate facial expressions.
- Use the stickman as a scale reference, witness-like viewpoint, or quiet guide when it helps the story.
- Do not put the stickman in every shot. Locations, objects, maps, documents, and atmospheric details remain the main visual focus.
- Do not portray the stickman as a real witness or as proof that a depicted event happened.
- In sensitive or tragic reconstructions, avoid graphic depiction; use symbolic silhouettes and clearly label AI reconstruction/illustration.

## General review checklist
1. Does the character match the correct channel identity?
2. Is the design consistent in proportions, line weight, and key features?
3. Does the character support the story rather than distract from it?
4. Are historical/folklore reconstructions clearly labelled where needed?
5. Has any design detail from another channel accidentally leaked in?

If a mismatch is significant, regenerate or flag for human review. Never silently redefine an approved character.
