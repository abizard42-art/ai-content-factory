# Story Research

## Purpose
Create a traceable research brief for either Sejarah Dunia or Peta Sunyi. Research is not the same as writing: do not turn an interesting claim into a fact merely because it makes a stronger story.

## Required process
1. Identify the exact claim, person, event, building, location, and relevant date range.
2. Prefer primary sources, official archives, museums, academic publications, reputable news organizations, and local-government records.
3. Verify important claims against at least two independent sources when practical. Prefer sources that directly support the exact claim.
4. Record the source title, publisher/author, publication date (if available), URL, and the claim it supports.
5. Separate:
   - verified facts;
   - attributed folklore, oral history, or popular accounts;
   - disputed interpretations;
   - unknown or unverified details.
6. For location stories, verify the place name and map position independently. Do not invent coordinates, street numbers, witnesses, quotations, or dates.
7. If web research is unavailable, say so plainly and produce a research-needed brief. Never pretend to have searched.

## Channel rules
### Sejarah Dunia
- Prioritize established historical evidence and clearly label scholarly debates.
- Check chronology, geography, names, titles, and cultural context.
- Do not present legends as documented history.

### Peta Sunyi
- Distinguish documented place history from local legend and supernatural claims.
- Never present paranormal claims as proven facts.
- Do not invent eyewitnesses or attribute a quote to an unnamed resident.
- Do not encourage trespassing, dangerous exploration, or disturbance of residents.

## Output format
Return:
- topic and research question;
- concise summary;
- verified facts (each with source references);
- legends/disputed claims (with attribution and evidence status);
- unknowns and verification gaps;
- source list with URLs and relevance;
- editorial cautions;
- recommendation: ready for script draft or needs more research.

## Quality gate
A claim without a traceable source must not be labelled verified. If key facts cannot be checked, mark the brief as insufficient_sources and explain what evidence is missing.
