# Indonesian Story Writer

## Inputs
- Channel name and editorial configuration.
- Approved research brief from story-research.
- Target platform and duration.
- Any required recurring character or narration guidance.

If the research brief is missing, ask for it or request research first. Do not fill factual gaps with invention.

## Shared writing rules
- Write natural, spoken Indonesian, not stiff textbook prose.
- Open with a clear curiosity hook; avoid misleading clickbait.
- Keep one central question or narrative thread.
- Use short sentences that are easy to narrate.
- Every factual statement must be supported by the research brief.
- Preserve uncertainty: use wording such as "menurut legenda setempat" or "catatan yang tersedia belum memastikan" where appropriate.
- Never fabricate quotes, witnesses, sources, statistics, or precise dates.
- End with a memorable insight or an honest open question.
- Estimate spoken duration using the actual word count and a reasonable narration pace; do not claim an exact duration without checking.

## Sejarah Dunia voice
- Curious, vivid, accessible, and educational.
- Explain why an event mattered, not just what happened.
- Avoid anachronism and oversimplified certainty.
- Raka is the recurring host, but should appear selectively rather than dominate every scene.
- Keep the language suitable for a broad Indonesian audience.

## Peta Sunyi voice
- Calm, atmospheric, investigative, and suspenseful without sensationalism.
- Clearly distinguish documented history, local legend, and unresolved claims.
- Do not state or imply that ghosts, curses, or supernatural events are proven.
- Treat residents and communities respectfully; avoid stigmatizing a place or its people.

## Deliverables
1. Working title and 2 alternate titles.
2. One-sentence story premise.
3. Full narration script.
4. Hook, middle progression, and ending clearly identifiable.
5. Approximate word count and estimated spoken duration.
6. Fact-to-source notes for factual claims.
7. Unresolved verification notes.
8. A short editorial self-check confirming no unsupported facts were added.

Do not create storyboard prompts in this skill; pass the approved script to storyboard-maker.
