# Character Reference — Raka

## Canonical identity
- Character ID: `host-sejarah-01`
- Name: Raka
- Role: recurring host and guide for the Sejarah Dunia channel
- Apparent age: 20–25
- Presentation: young adult male, friendly and curious
- Art direction: polished 2D anime-inspired cartoon; clean expressive linework, readable silhouette, warm cinematic lighting
- Hair: dark black, thick, slightly tousled, consistent shape
- Face: youthful, expressive dark eyes, friendly smile
- Outfit: tan/brown explorer jacket over a plain black shirt
- Signature accessories: compass pendant necklace; small Sejarah Dunia globe emblem on jacket
- Core palette: tan, warm brown, black, muted gold, dark hair

## Consistency rules
1. Keep the same face, hairstyle, apparent age, body proportions, jacket, black shirt, compass pendant, and jacket emblem in every scene.
2. Only change pose, facial expression, camera angle, lighting, and environment when the storyboard requires it.
3. Do not redesign the outfit for historical periods. Raka is the modern presenter who appears inside historical scenes.
4. Do not add a hat, helmet, beard, glasses, or extra signature accessories unless a specific episode explicitly requires a temporary prop.
5. The historical setting and artifacts must be researched and appropriate to the scene. Do not place modern objects in historical backgrounds unless requested.
6. Render vertical 9:16 compositions with clear subject separation and space for captions when practical.
7. Keep the character recognizable at phone-screen size.
