# Peta Sunyi — Channel Configuration

## Identity
- Channel name: **Peta Sunyi**
- Handle: `@PetaSunyiCerita`
- Tagline: *Ada cerita di balik setiap tempat..*
- Channel slug: `peta-sunyi`
- Language: Indonesian
- Initial coverage: Bandung, West Java
- Expansion: all of Indonesia
- Format: YouTube Shorts, vertical 9:16
- Initial target: 2 videos/day
- Initial duration target: 60–90 seconds
- Visual approach: hybrid (real location imagery + AI folklore reconstructions + maps/archive material)

## Editorial format
1. Hook: a strange detail or question.
2. Place: identify the real location and its documented history.
3. Legend: attribute folklore to the people/publications who tell it.
4. Evidence: distinguish documented facts from unverified claims.
5. Close: leave a thoughtful question; do not claim supernatural proof.

## Visual sourcing
- Real photographs: use only images with a clear reuse license or explicit permission; preserve source URL and attribution.
- AI reconstructions: label/describe them as illustrations or reconstructions, never as documentary photographs.
- Maps and archive material: verify dates, location, and reuse terms.
- Never scrape copyrighted images or imply an AI image is an authentic photograph.

## Automation stages
1. Research and source collection.
2. Script and storyboard generation, saved as a draft in Supabase.
3. Human fact-check and approval.
4. Visual sourcing and image generation.
5. Indonesian narration generation.
6. 9:16 rendering, subtitles, sound design, and quality check.
7. Manual approval before publishing.

Auto-upload remains disabled until the entire pipeline is tested.
